# Looking for a developer experience engineer

- Source: <https://blog.janestreet.com/looking-for-a-developer-experience-engineer-index/>
- Published: 2021-06-15
- Kind: blog post

## Summary

- Job advert (since filled) from the Tools and Compilers team for a developer experience
  engineer acting as liaison between the tools team and the firm's developers.
- About half the role is hands-on tool work: editor plugins (Ecaml, Vcaml), the Iron code
  review system and the Dune build system.
- The rest is rotating through teams to learn their workflows, onboarding them to new tools,
  teaching through the bootcamp, talks, tutorials and demos, and feeding user needs into
  prioritization.
- Desired traits: love of engineering craft, strong writing, relationship building, and
  satisfaction from helping others.
