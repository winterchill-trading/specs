# We're sponsoring SoME3

- Source: <https://blog.janestreet.com/were-sponsoring-some3/>
- Published: 2023-07-06
- Kind: blog post

## Summary

- Jane Street announces sponsorship of SoME3, the third Summer of Math Exposition contest run
  by the creator of the 3Blue1Brown channel and a co-organizer, which encourages new math
  education videos.
- The post praises a problem-first style of teaching, where definitions emerge from the
  structure a question needs, over presenting mathematics in finished form.
- The contest aims to grow and surface a curated pool of math content and a more diverse set
  of creators.
- The firm ties this to its own practice of hiring curious people and teaching them the
  math, programming and trading they need.
