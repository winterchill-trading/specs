# Learn OCaml in NYC

- Source: <https://blog.janestreet.com/learn-ocaml-nyc/>
- Published: 2018-02-16
- Kind: blog post

## Summary

- Announces a free one-day OCaml workshop in New York on March 24, 2018, 10 AM to 4 PM,
  with lunch provided; registration closed March 16 and space was limited.
- Audience: effective programmers who do not know OCaml or anything like it, with or
  without functional programming experience.
- Content: exercises on the language basics plus current tooling, namely dune for builds,
  Merlin for editor support, expect tests, and js_of_ocaml for the browser.
- Setup instructions and a mailing list were promised so installation happens beforehand.
- An advanced track (for example the Incremental library) would run if enough experienced
  users registered; dune and expect tests were pitched as worth learning even for them.
