# Learn OCaml in NYC

- Source: <https://blog.janestreet.com/learn-ocaml-nyc/>
- Published: 2018-02-16
- Kind: blog post

## Summary

- Announces a free one-day OCaml workshop in New York on March 24 (2018), 10 AM to 4 PM.
- Aimed at competent programmers who do not know OCaml or similar languages.
- Built around exercises on language basics plus modern tooling: dune for builds, Merlin
  for editor support, expect tests, and js_of_ocaml for the browser.
- Participants should set up their platform in advance; an advanced track (for example on
  Incremental) was offered if enough experienced users registered.

## Relevance to us

- No relevance beyond confirming the dune, Merlin and expect test toolset we already use.
- **Action:** none.
