# What's 2013 + 50? 1969, of course

- Source: <https://blog.janestreet.com/whats-2013-50-1969-of-course/>
- Published: 2013-09-11
- Kind: blog post

## Summary

- Generating a self-signed CA certificate with GnuTLS `certtool` 2.8.5 (CentOS 6.4) and an
  18250-day (50-year) validity produced a "Not After" date of 31 December 1969.
- FreeBSD's 2.12.23 build showed the same bug with 1 January 1970; GnuTLS 3.2.4 built from
  source produced the correct 2063 date.
- The symptom matches an overflow of a signed 32-bit seconds counter past 2038, wrapping to
  a value at or before the epoch.
- Distributions were shipping much older GnuTLS major versions, possibly because of ABI
  changes, so the fix had not reached them.

## Relevance to us

- A reminder about time-range overflow; our timestamps are 63-bit `int` nanoseconds or `Time_ns.t`,
  good until about 2116, and venue milliseconds are converted at the edge in the protocol libraries,
  so nothing here applies directly.
- **Action:** none.
