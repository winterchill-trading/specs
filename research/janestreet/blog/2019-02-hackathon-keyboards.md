# Machining the ultimate hackathon prize

- Source: <https://blog.janestreet.com/hackathon-keyboards/>
- Published: 2019-02-28
- Kind: blog post

## Summary

- Jane Street sponsored the MakeMIT hackathon and built custom 68-key mechanical keyboards
  as the prize: Cherry MX Brown switches, a CNC-machined walnut case and brass plates.
- The electronics came off the shelf: a Tada68 PCB running the QMK firmware, with switches
  snapped into a laser-cut 1.5 mm brass plate and soldered through-hole.
- Most effort went into the case: a Rhino model exported to Fusion 360, cut on a desktop CNC
  router with facing, adaptive clearing and a second finer clearing pass, plus an angled
  facing pass for a 5-degree typing slope.
- Threaded inserts hold the PCB, keeping part count low.
- The decorative bottom plate carries a logo generated by a small OCaml DSL over signed
  distance functions (shapes as functions from a point to edge distance, combined with
  min for union), sampled with marching squares into polylines for CAM software.
