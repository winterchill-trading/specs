# What the SP interns have wrought, 2026 edition

- Source: <https://blog.janestreet.com/wrought-2026-sp/>
- Published: 2026-09-30
- Kind: blog post

## Summary

- Strategy and Product interns sit on a team with a mentor and work on business-facing
  problems that need both analysis and some building.
- Financing costs: modelling fees on four kinds of loan arrangements and allocating them
  back to desks and strategies; the post notes that allocating by gross position size
  would unfairly penalize large but cheap-to-finance positions.
- Risk limits on unusual dates: every order passes automatic risk checks whose limits
  follow a time-of-day schedule, with trader overrides for events such as central bank
  meetings or the mismatched New York and London daylight-saving changes.
- That project found overrides that expired unnoticed and curtailed trading at important
  moments, and proposed owner and lifecycle metadata with automatic expiry reminders, plus
  separating mechanical overrides (clock changes) from substantive ones with different
  review cycles.
- Shorter items: rule-based host grouping for fleet-wide kernel migrations, a study of an
  internal Slack-to-agent tool that added a plan-review step before the agent starts, and
  late-vendor-data alerting for reference data.

## Relevance to us

- Weak. The closest idea is time-scheduled risk limits with owned, expiring overrides,
  which [mm-core](../../../repos/mm-core.md) risk checks do not need until we trade real
  money on a schedule.
