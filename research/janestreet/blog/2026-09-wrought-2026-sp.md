# What the SP interns have wrought, 2026 edition

- Source: <https://blog.janestreet.com/wrought-2026-sp/>
- Published: 2026-09-30
- Kind: blog post

## Summary

- Strategy and Product specialists combine "why is this valuable" thinking with detailed
  analysis, working on desks, in infrastructure and with tech teams; interns join a team
  with a mentor and take on real projects.
- Financing costs: a model of fee expense on four kinds of loan arrangements, allocated
  back to desks and strategies. Allocation choice changes how profitable the same trading
  looks; allocating by gross position size, for instance, would penalize large positions
  that are cheap to finance. The intern compared modelling choices on data and drove
  consensus across teams.
- Risk limits on unusual dates: every order passes automated risk checks (open order
  counts, maximum order size and so on) whose limits follow a time-of-day schedule. Traders
  add overrides for events such as central bank meetings, employment reports, and the
  different New York and London daylight-saving dates.
- That project interviewed traders and compared historical limits with usage. It found
  overrides that expired unnoticed and curtailed trading at important moments, and proposed
  owner and lifecycle metadata on the shared special-dates dictionary with automatic expiry
  reminders.
- It also separated mechanical overrides (clock changes) from substantive ones, each with
  its own review cycle, to keep reminders high-signal.
- Shorter items: codifying host-class grouping for fleet-wide kernel upgrades from hardware
  specs and usage metrics; a study of an internal tool that turns a Slack thread into an
  asynchronous agent task, which found agents often needed direction first, leading to a
  plan-review exchange in Slack before work starts; and alerting for late vendor reference
  data, prioritized by which consumers care.

## Relevance to us

- **Market making and trading systems:** risk overrides need an owner and an expiry, or
  they silently lapse or linger. **Action:** when per-run overrides are added to
  [mm-core](../../../repos/mm-core.md) `Params`, require an expiry time and have `validate`
  reject an expired override at startup.
- **Research tooling:** agents started from a chat message produce more usable changes
  when a human shapes the plan first. Context for our own agent workflow; no repo change.
- Loan financing allocation does not apply to a perpetuals stack (funding is explicit).
