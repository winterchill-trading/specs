# The Jane Street Interview Process — 2020 Edition

- Source: <https://blog.janestreet.com/jane-street-interview-process-2020/>
- Published: 2020-07-24
- Kind: blog post

## Summary

- Describes the software engineer hiring process for interns, new graduates and experienced
  hires: one technical phone screen, then two to four technical on-site rounds (held over
  video during COVID-19).
- Rounds are coding and algorithms problems with one or two engineers, aimed at seeing how a
  candidate works and communicates; mistakes are expected.
- Tooling: CoderPad for coding, Webex for video; London adds a HackerRank round before the
  phone screen for capacity reasons.
- Experienced candidates may get a non-technical call and experience-based on-site rounds.
- Myths corrected: no math or probability questions for engineering roles, no bonus for
  using OCaml, no need to know OCaml or finance, no advanced degree required.
