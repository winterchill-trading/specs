# Introducing the Jane Street Graduate Research Fellowship

- Source: <https://blog.janestreet.com/graduate-research-fellowship/>
- Published: 2022-08-30
- Kind: blog post

## Summary

- Jane Street launches a one-year fellowship for PhD students in mathematics, computer
  science or statistics, describing its trading as combining machine learning, distributed
  systems, programmable hardware, statistics and applied mathematics.
- The award covers full tuition and fees for the academic year plus a $40,000 taxable
  stipend, and fellows are invited, all expenses paid, to give a talk in New York.
- Eligible students are in years 2 to 6 of a full-time PhD at an accredited US
  doctorate-granting university and must stay in good standing for the year; deferrals are
  considered case by case.
- Fellowships need university-specific legal agreements; the firm has some in place and will
  negotiate with other universities for accepted fellows.
