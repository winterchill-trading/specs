# Research internships in our Tools and Compilers group

- Source: <https://blog.janestreet.com/research-internships-tnc/>
- Published: 2022-03-04
- Kind: blog post

## Summary

- Jane Street announces research internships in its Tools and Compilers group for PhD
  students in programming languages, compilers, verification and related areas, run
  alongside the undergraduate internship and hosted in London and New York year-round.
- The Compilers team works on making OCaml more expressive, its generated code faster and
  the compiler itself faster and easier to use, upstreaming as much as possible; the wider
  group also builds profiling, debugging, documentation and build tools.
- Recent work cited: unboxed types, modal types for safe stack allocation, software
  prefetching in the GC that sped up marking substantially, an evaluation of the best-fit
  allocator, magic-trace, memtrace, build systems, UI frameworks and optimization.
- Example project areas: type systems for locality and uniqueness, verifying C bindings
  against the OCaml GC's rules, and super-optimization.
- Projects are chosen with each candidate to match their research, aim for publication, and
  may continue after the internship because the work is open source.

## Relevance to us

- Weak: a hiring post; the listed research directions (locality and uniqueness modes) are
  covered in more depth by the Oxidizing OCaml notes, and we write no C bindings of our own.
