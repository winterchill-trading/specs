# Do applied programming languages research at Jane Street

- Source: <https://blog.janestreet.com/applied-PL-research/>
- Published: 2019-08-16
- Kind: blog post

## Summary

- Hiring post for compiler engineers with a programming languages research background.
- Current projects listed: unboxed types, feedback-directed optimization, typed algebraic
  effects and intermediate representations for inlining and optimization.
- Possible future work: modular implicits, macros and staging, Rust-style ownership, and
  inductive families in the module system.
- Several of these later surfaced in OxCaml (unboxed types, modes inspired by ownership).
