# Advent of FPGA — A Jane Street Challenge

- Source: <https://blog.janestreet.com/advent-of-fpga-challenge-2025/>
- Published: 2025-11-24
- Kind: blog post

## Summary

- Announcement of a community challenge alongside Advent of Code 2025: solve any number
  of puzzles as synthesizable RTL with realistic I/O, ideally in Hardcaml, following an
  earlier post that solved the 2024 puzzles in Hardcaml.
- Rules: open-source code plus testbench and a write-up, realistic resource use (running on
  an FPGA is optional), any RTL language but no high-level synthesis, original work and no
  obviously AI-generated entries; deadline 2026-01-16.
- Suggested directions: scaling to much larger inputs, area and performance trade-offs,
  FPGA-native parallelism and pipelining, open ASIC flows such as Tiny Tapeout, and
  showcasing HDL language features.
- Prizes: three Kria KV260 FPGA kits for the most inventive entries, and a T-shirt for
  anyone completing a puzzle in Hardcaml; a template project and tutorials are linked.
