# How Jane Street Pairs Interns to Projects and Teams During the Software Engineering Internship

- Source: <https://blog.janestreet.com/project-pairing/>
- Published: 2022-01-14
- Kind: blog post

## Summary

- Intern projects are proposed by would-be mentors from their teams' real needs and reviewed
  by the developers who run the internship program.
- Accepted projects must be genuinely wanted (no busywork), get the intern writing code
  early for fast feedback, and have several releasable milestones so some work reaches
  production even if the whole project does not finish.
- Before the internship, each intern has a call with an engineer from the program about
  preferences: area of the firm, kind of technical work, team dynamics and working style,
  things to avoid, and how strongly each preference matters.
- Interns do not pick from a project list because contested projects are hard to allocate
  fairly, short descriptions lack context, team fit often matters more than project content,
  and projects are only defined in the weeks before the start.
- Past projects are summarized in the yearly "What the interns have wrought" posts.

## Relevance to us

- None for our code; the "releasable milestones early" criterion is a general project
  hygiene point we already follow with small, frequently pushed commits.
- **Action:** none.
