# Hiring a Developer Educator

- Source: <https://blog.janestreet.com/hiring-a-developer-educator/>
- Published: 2021-10-21
- Kind: blog post

## Summary

- Jane Street runs many internal courses: OCaml Bootcamp for new traders, trading classes for
  non-traders, Python for traders and researchers, a first-year cycle of advanced developer
  classes, and classes for interns and candidates.
- All of that teaching was done by staff on top of their regular work; the firm wants its
  first full-time educator with deep teaching experience.
- The role needs a skilled teacher who also knows the technology stack well, so a
  significant share of the time goes to writing code.
- Beyond teaching and building curricula, the educator is expected to mentor other staff who
  teach; university and industry teaching backgrounds are both welcome.
