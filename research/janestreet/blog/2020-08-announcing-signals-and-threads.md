# Announcing Signals and Threads, a new podcast from Jane Street

- Source: <https://blog.janestreet.com/announcing-signals-and-threads-index/>
- Published: 2020-08-31
- Kind: blog post

## Summary

- Launch post for Signals and Threads, a weekly podcast replacing in-person tech talks during
  the pandemic.
- Format: the host interviews one Jane Street engineer per episode, going deep on their area.
- Announced topics include language design, clock synchronization, IP multicast in markets
  and reconfigurable hardware; the first episode covers Hardcaml.
- Originally aimed at recruiting, it turned out to interest internal staff as well.

## Relevance to us

- An index pointer only: the episodes themselves (clocks, multicast, exchange design) are the
  useful sources and get their own notes. **Action:** none.
