# Research notes

Reading notes on public engineering writing from a trading firm whose stack ours is built
on: the Jane Street tech blog and the Signals and Threads podcast. The goal is to pull out
what matters for this organization (trading system and data platform architecture,
OCaml and OxCaml practice, testing, performance, deployment, research tooling) and turn it
into concrete proposals for our repositories.

## How to read

- **Per-source notes** live under [janestreet/blog/](janestreet/blog/) (named
  `<yyyy-mm>-<slug>.md`) and [janestreet/podcast/](janestreet/podcast/) (named
  `<nn>-<slug>.md`, by episode number). Each note gives the source URL and date, a short
  summary in our own words, a "Relevance to us" section naming the affected repositories
  and a concrete action where there is one, and a tags line.
- **Theme documents** under [themes/](themes/) synthesize across sources. Read these
  first; follow their links into individual notes for detail.
- **Top 20** below is the short list of insights we think are worth acting on, each
  linked to its note and the repository it affects. The same actions appear one line each
  in [roadmap.md](../roadmap.md#from-research).
- Notes summarize; they do not reproduce. Quotes are short and attributed. Read the
  original for anything load-bearing.

## Scope

- Every Signals and Threads episode.
- Every blog post from 2018 onward, and earlier posts whose title is relevant to
  trading systems, OCaml practice, testing, performance or tooling.
