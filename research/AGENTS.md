# Research (AGENTS.md)

Reading notes and studies that feed proposals for the organization's repositories. Each subdirectory
groups one study: `banking-infrastructure/`, `janestreet/`, `opam-readiness/`, `oxcaml-performance/`,
and single notes such as `hl-sim-parity.md`. `README.md` lists them.

- A note records its sources (title, author or publisher, link, date read) and what it concludes for us.
  Quote sparingly; summarize in your own words.
- A proposal derived from a study names the repository it affects and links the `repos/` page.
- Benchmark results carry the machine class, the compiler switch and the commit they were measured on.
- Notes are not specifications. Move a settled conclusion into `design/` or an ADR and link back.
- Keep the research index in `README.md` current when a file is added or moved.
